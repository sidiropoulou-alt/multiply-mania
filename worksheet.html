Part B — Challenges

  1. 21.  7 × 40 = 280 pencils
    (Think: 7 × 4 = 28, then add a zero.)
  2. 22.  The missing number is 70. Check: 6 × 70 = 420.
    (Think: make 420 a friendly number — 42 ÷ 6 = 7, then add a zero.)
  3. 23.  30 × 5 = 150, then 150 + 20 = 170 pages.
  4. 24.  3 × 200 = 600.
    (The pattern: 3 × 2 = 6, then add two zeros.)
  5. 25.  8 × 40 = 320  and  6 × 50 = 300. So 8 × 40 is greater by 20.
    (Think: 8 × 4 = 32 → 320, and 6 × 5 = 30 → 300.)
